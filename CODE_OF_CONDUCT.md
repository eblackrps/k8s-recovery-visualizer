# Code Of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We want `k8s-recovery-visualizer` to be a welcoming, professional project for operators, contributors, reviewers, and users of all experience levels.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, respectful, and harassment-free community.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Assume good intent, but communicate clearly when something is broken or risky.
- Focus feedback on the work and the impact, not the person.
- Welcome questions from people who are learning the repo or the domain.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, or abusive language
- Personal attacks, trolling, or intimidation
- Sharing private information without permission
- Sustained disruptive behavior in issues, PRs, or discussions

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing this Code of Conduct. They may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions that violate these standards.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, contact the maintainer privately through GitHub.
